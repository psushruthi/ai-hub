# AI Hub concept

A concept for one front door to AI and data science across the school: people, events, learning, collaboration, resources, help routing, impact and a 90-day launch plan.

Built by Sushruthi Panakanti from public information on CCBB and IU websites (October 2026). Not an official Indiana University website.

## How the content works

All content lives in a spreadsheet with six tabs: `people`, `events`, `learn`, `resources`, `posts`, `kpis`.

- Saved copies are in the `data` folder.
- To make the site update live from Google Sheets, paste your sheet ID into `config.js`.
- In lists such as `data_types`, separate items with a semicolon (`;`).
- `status` can be `Existing`, `Scheduled`, `Proposed` or `Example`.
